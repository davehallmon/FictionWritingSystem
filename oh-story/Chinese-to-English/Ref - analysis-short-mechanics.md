# Analysis of Core Mechanisms in Short-Form Source Text

> Analyze how a short story's central hook is tested, varied, and resolved within its limited total length. Here, “cycle” means only a finite recurrence of the same mechanism within the source and carries no requirement for long-term expansion.

## Central Question

A core mechanism is not a genre label or the name of a special advantage. It is the choice a character repeatedly uses to handle conflict and the continuing expectation this creates for the reader.

Express it in one sentence:

```text
当 {触发条件} 出现，角色通过 {关键选择/手段} 获得 {局部结果}，同时承担 {限制/代价}；终局由 {不可逆选择} 结算。
```

**English guide (non-executable):** When {trigger condition} occurs, the character obtains {local result} through {key choice or method} while accepting {constraint or cost}; the ending is settled by {irreversible choice}.

## Mechanism Evidence Matrix

| Dimension | Observational question | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger | What event activates the mechanism? | Location of its first appearance and preconditions |
| Input | What must the character invest? | Time, relationships, reputation, resources, secrets, or choices |
| Process | Which step of the solution does the mechanism change? | Visible action, not only explanatory worldbuilding |
| Output | What local result does each operation deliver? | Change in information, relationships, risk, resources, or identity |
| Limitation | When does the mechanism fail or become unusable? | Failure, expiration, conflicting rule, or real-world resistance |
| Cost | Who pays for progress, and does the cost remain on the same central plotline? | Actual consequences after operation |
| Variation | What changes between a later use and the previous one? | Change in target, interpretation, choice, or cost |
| Resolution | Does the ending still require a human choice? | Final irreversible action and its consequences |

## Types of Finite Recurrence

| Type | Test | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Single trigger | The mechanism changes one crucial point, after which the story handles consequences | Excessive explanation of the premise crowds out character choice |
| Progressive recurrence | The same rule appears several times, each increasing cost or changing interpretation | Raising only numeric values without changing choices feels repetitive |
| Evidence relay | The mechanism successively changes the source, interpretation, and ownership of evidence | Evidence that does not affect action becomes accumulated reference material |
| Relationship relay | The same conflict reveals new responsibility across different relationships | New characters introduced only to provide answers dilute the central plot |
| Correction of misjudgment | A character forms a provisional answer from the mechanism and later evidence corrects it | A later layer that invalidates every earlier fact feels like cheating |
| Corresponding retribution | Earlier harms and later consequences create item-by-item causal echoes | Coincidental punishment weakens character agency |

## System-Based and High-Concept Short Fiction

When a system, ability, or abnormal rule appears, also check:

- Is the rule understandable before its first crucial use?
- Are later changes consequences of established rules or temporary new features?
- Does the ability solve a local obstacle, or does it perform evidentiary, relational, and ethical choices for the character?
- After the ability exits, transfers, or fails, do character actions still resolve the central plot?
- Does the ending fulfill the opening promise, or abruptly turn into a display of wealth, combat power, or a larger world?

A system may still form the core mechanism of a short story when it triggers only a few times and its rules do not upgrade. Do not therefore classify it as “lacking long-term potential”; short fiction carries no obligation for a long-running cycle.

## Protagonist Agency

At every decisive point, record four roles: who obtains the information, who verifies it, who interprets it, and who chooses to use it. The protagonist need not own every action but must make an irreplaceable choice affecting final responsibility or relationships.

If a system, ally, antagonist confession, or coincidence supplies every crucial answer, report that “the mechanism/supporting character takes causal authority” and cite the exact location. Do not use the vague statement “the protagonist is not proactive enough.”

## Relationship Between Mechanism and Genre

- Genre identifies what the story primarily settles; mechanism identifies the repeated causal process used to advance it.
- Suspense, systems, rebirth, and a disembodied viewpoint may be the primary genre or only a tool serving a family, workplace, or relationship plotline.
- If the central plot survives unchanged after deleting the mechanism, the mechanism is decorative.
- If deleting the genre conflict leaves the mechanism without an objective, the mechanism serves that genre's central plot.

## Report Format

```markdown
核心机制：{一句话}
运转证据：{按出现顺序列位置、输入、输出、代价}
有限复现类型：{类型及依据}
规则一致性：{成立/存在缺口/证据不足}
主角代理权：{取得、验证、解释、使用的所有权}
终局结算：{机制是否退出，人物选择是否接管}
可复用功能：{抽象功能}
失败条件：{移植时最容易失效的因果}
```

**English guide (non-executable):** Core mechanism; ordered evidence of inputs, outputs, and costs; bounded reproduction type; rule consistency; protagonist ownership of acquisition, verification, interpretation, and use; whether the mechanism exits at the ending and human choice takes over; reusable abstract function; and the causal link most likely to fail when transferred.

## Boundaries

- Do not evaluate whether the mechanism can support hundreds of thousands of Chinese characters, a number of volumes, or a number of upgrades.
- Do not require a fixed recurrence count. Record the source's actual count and whether each recurrence changes causality.
- Do not place writing recommendations, market-track selection, or innovation formulas in the decomposition report.
- When evidence is insufficient, mark “Requires verification”; do not invent rules or authorial intent for the source.
