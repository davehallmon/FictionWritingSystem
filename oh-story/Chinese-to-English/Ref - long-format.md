# Long-Form Manuscript Format

> Applies only to paragraphs, dialogue, punctuation, and engineering metadata in long-form chapters. Short-form conventions—the `###1.` section format, a complete-story structure of 8–15 sections, and a target of 500–800 Chinese characters per section—do not apply.

## Chapters and Paragraphs

- Follow the project's established format for chapter titles, numbers, and filenames. Do not repeat the chapter number inside the manuscript body.
- Start a new paragraph at natural dramatic units, shot changes, new actions, new information, or new dialogue. Do not divide paragraphs at fixed character counts.
- Short paragraphs are a pacing tool, not a default template. Complete reasoning, atmosphere, and emotional causality may use longer paragraphs.
- Write payoff beats, turns, and crucial choices densely. Give transitions, travel, and connections between known information less space. Do not make every beat the same length.
- Name the character at the beginning of a paragraph when necessary; within the paragraph, continue with pronouns, omission, or actions. Do not mechanically repeat the character's name in every sentence.
- Adjacent manuscript paragraphs must contain exactly one newline character, `\n`. Do not insert blank lines or consecutive newlines, `\n\n`.
- Do not indent manuscript paragraphs or begin them with `　　` (full-width spaces) or half-width spaces.

## Dialogue

- Follow the quotation-mark convention of the project or platform and use it consistently throughout the chapter. If none is specified, use half-width double quotation marks.
- Put dialogue on its own line. Place a character's accompanying actions before or after that character's speech without labeling every line “he said” or “she said.”
- Use question marks for genuine questions, challenges, or rhetorical questions. Reserve exclamation marks for emotional peaks and never repeat them consecutively.
- Convey pauses, hesitation, swallowing words, interruption, or drawn-out speech through actions, short sentences, line breaks, or incomplete sentences. Do not use ellipses or em dashes as universal pause markers in narration or dialogue.
- Every line of dialogue must change information, a relationship, strategy, or the balance of power. Convert long passages of exposition into questions and answers, probing exchanges, rebuttals, or consequences expressed through action.

## Long-Form Chapter Constraints

- The chapter-level `字数目标` and `visible_chars_v1` are the sole standards for character count. Use a ±12% internal tolerance and a ±15% user-facing tolerance, measured with deterministic tooling.
- If the chapter is short, first confirm that every approved plot event in the detailed chapter outline has been implemented. Do not pad the chapter with independent events, characters, rules, reversals, or foreshadowing.
- If the chapter is long, first remove repeated explanations, repeated reactions, process narration, and nonfunctional micro-actions. Do not remove approved plot events, causality, choices, payoffs, or hooks.
- A chapter may contain multiple scenes or beats; their number follows the detailed chapter outline and causal needs. Do not apply the short-form story's section count.

## Isolating Engineering Metadata

- Outside the title line, narration, dialogue, and interiority must not contain writing-engineering terms such as “this chapter,” “previous chapter,” “earlier text,” “later text,” “foreshadowing,” “detailed outline,” or “reader.”
- When continuity is required, use an event anchor or relative time that the character can perceive. The exception is a character who genuinely discusses chapters or reader identity within the story world.
- Do not use bold, italics, horizontal rules, or review reports in the manuscript body. A valid chapter title is exempt.

## Delivery Checklist

- [ ] Paragraph breaks follow changes in shots and actions rather than a fixed sentence-count template; there are no blank lines or indentation between paragraphs.
- [ ] Quotation marks, question marks, exclamation marks, and pause conventions are consistent throughout the chapter.
- [ ] Long-form length follows the chapter outline and deterministic character-count standard, without applying short-form section thresholds.
- [ ] The manuscript contains no writing-engineering metadata, self-checks, or explanatory notes.
