# Chapter-Level Hooks: Choosing and Writing Chapter-Opening and Chapter-Ending Hooks

> Load when writing the beginning or ending of a chapter. First consult the decision router, choose the right hook type, then apply a template.

---

## Decision Router

### Chapter-Ending Hooks: Choose by This Chapter's Emotion

| Emotion/stage of this chapter | Recommended hook | Why |
|-------------|---------|-------|
| Suspense/unknown | Sudden reveal / mysterious object / omission | Arouses curiosity |
| Urgency/crisis | Immediate crisis / countdown / interrupted action | Creates anxiety |
| Reversal/truth | Identity reversal / hidden meaning / echo | Overturns assumptions |
| Emotion/relationship | Dilemma / promise or threat / emotional turn | Creates emotional tension |
| System-fiction-specific | System activation / choice | Creates anticipation for the special advantage |
| Atmosphere/daily life | Image hook / bizarre disappearance / information gap | Suggests an undercurrent |

### Chapter-Opening Hooks: Choose by Opening Strategy

| Opening strategy | Recommended hook | Why |
|---------|---------|-------|
| Direct impact | Begin with interrupted action / begin with a countdown | Immediate urgency |
| Create curiosity | Suspenseful dialogue / flash-forward fragment / mysterious monologue | Driven by an information gap |
| Contrasting impact | Contrasting scenes / portentous image | Contrast attracts attention |

### Story Stage and Hook Strength

| Story stage | Recommended type | Strength |
|----------|---------|------|
| Chapter 1 | System activation / cutoff / escalating crisis | Must be strong |
| Chapters 2–3 | Choice / information gap / emotional turn | Strong |
| Mid-story daily life | Suspenseful dialogue / information gap / imminent identity reveal | Medium |
| Before the climax | Escalating crisis / suspenseful foreknowledge | Strong |
| Finale | Time jump / emotional turn | Closure |

## Thirteen Chapter-Ending Hooks

| # | Name | Formula | Example |
|---|------|------|------|
| 1 | Sudden reveal | Introduce information that changes the entire situation | “The date on the letter was the seventh day after his death.” |
| 2 | Immediate crisis | An urgent threat the next chapter must address | “The crack was widening, and they were still three spirit stones short.” |
| 3 | Interrupted action | A new variable interrupts an action | “He had just reached out when someone suddenly seized his wrist. ‘Don't move.’ A voice came from behind him.” |
| 4 | Identity reversal | The truth of an identity departs from reader expectations | “Her name was Lin Xiaoyue. But the file said: Lin Xiaoyue, deceased.” |
| 5 | Dilemma | Force a choice between two bad options | “If he signed this document, the company would survive—but he would go to prison.” |
| 6 | Mysterious object | An important object whose meaning is unknown | “Inside the package was a key and a note: ‘You owe me.’” |
| 7 | Countdown | There is not enough time | “The doctor said three months. That was two months ago.” |
| 8 | Promise/threat | Someone declares an intention to act | “Before midnight tonight, I will tell everyone what you did.” |
| 9 | Bizarre disappearance | An impossible disappearance | “The handcuffs remained, but the person was gone. The room had one door, and it had never opened.” |
| 10 | Hidden meaning | Apparently normal words conceal information | “He said, ‘You look so much like your younger sister.’ But she was an only child.” |
| 11 | Image hook | A recurring image changes | “The jasmine on the windowsill had been dead for a month. Suddenly, it produced a bud.” |
| 12 | Echo hook | The final sentence echoes the opening, but a key detail changes | Opening: “She said she would never forgive him.” / Ending: “She said she would never forgive him. But her hand clenched tighter.” |
| 13 | Omission hook | Show only a reaction without revealing what happened | “He read the letter. His face changed. He said nothing, folded it, and put it in his pocket.” |

## Seven Chapter-Opening Hooks

| # | Name | Formula | Example |
|---|------|------|------|
| 1 | Suspenseful-dialogue opening | Begin directly with meaningful dialogue | “Are you sure you want to do this? / Yes. / Then don't regret it.” |
| 2 | Flash-forward fragment | Show a fragment of the result before returning to the normal narrative | “Only later did he learn that the call changed everything. At this moment, he still knew nothing.” |
| 3 | Countdown opening | Establish urgency immediately | “The contract expires in 72 hours.” |
| 4 | Mysterious monologue | An uncanny first-person internal monologue | “I keep wondering whether everything would have been different if I had not looked back that day.” |
| 5 | Contrasting scenes | Juxtapose two completely different scenes | “On one side, a wedding with a towering champagne display. On the other, lights flickering in a hospital corridor.” |
| 6 | Interrupted-action opening | Interrupt an action already in progress | “He had just inserted the key into the lock when a faint sound came from behind the door.” |
| 7 | Portentous image | Use an environmental image to suggest what is coming | “The horizon burned crimson, as though something were on fire. She glanced up and kept walking.” |

## Practical Chapter-Ending Templates

### 1. System Activation
```
就在{主角}{最低谷状态}之际。
{一个感官描写}。
叮！
【{系统名}，正式启动。】
```
> Just as he sank into utter despair, a cold mechanical voice suddenly sounded in his mind. Ding! 【The Reckless Youth System is now online.】

### 2. Suspenseful Foreknowledge
```
{主角}{冷静/冷漠地}看着{对方的嚣张行为}。
{一个短句：继续吧/慢慢来/不着急}。
{预告即将发生的反转，不透露细节}。
```
> I watched coldly as they doubled over laughing. Go on. Laugh a little longer. After today, none of you will ever laugh again.

### 3. Cutoff
```
{一个小动作}。
{剧烈反应}。
{震惊/疑问。本章结束，不给答案}。
```
> One bite! Her pupils contracted to needle points. Boom! An indescribably terrifying current of heat exploded inside her abdomen! Something was wrong with the congee! What had been put in it?

### 4. Choice
```
【选择一：{诱人选项}】奖励：{极其诱人} 代价：{隐含风险}
【选择二：{另一选项}】奖励：{另一种诱人} 代价：{另一种风险}
```
End the chapter without revealing the choice.

### 5. Escalating Crisis
```
{突然的声响/动作}！
{新威胁出现}。
{主角的恐惧/紧迫反应}。
```
> “All of you, get out here! Now!” Clang!!! A mud-caked military boot violently kicked open the cage door.

### 6. Imminent Identity Reveal
```
{一个旁观者}突然{提出疑问/发现线索}。
"{暗示主角真实身份的话}"
{其他人不以为意，主角暗暗紧张}。
```

### 7. Emotional Turn
```
就在这时，{角色}的{表情/态度}突然{大变}。
{一个出人意料的示弱/请求动作}。
"{戳中主角软肋的话}"
```

### 8. Information Gap
```
{主角的自我怀疑/感慨}。
{一个意象/比喻}。
{暗示即将改变，但不明说}。
```
> Flowers may bloom again, but youth never returns. How had he managed to turn his life into such a goddamn mess?

### 9. Suspenseful Dialogue
```
"{提出请求的前半句}"
"{对方回应}"
"{意想不到的后半句，不是读者预期的}"
```
> “I have a request,” Su Mu said slowly. “Go ahead.” “Before the divorce, I want to prove myself once.”

### 10. Time Jump
```
{精确/模糊的时间标记}。
{出人意料的结果}。
{暗示过程的一句话}。
```
> Three minutes and three seconds later, Jiang Yiyao walked calmly out of the room and straightened her slightly disheveled clothes.

### English guide (non-executable)

The ten protected formulas use these placeholders:

1. **System activation:** at the protagonist's lowest point; a sensory detail; “Ding!”; the named system formally activates.
2. **Suspenseful foreknowledge:** the protagonist watches the opponent's arrogance calmly or coldly; a short “go on,” “take your time,” or “no hurry”; then a reversal is foreshadowed without revealing details.
3. **Cutoff:** a small action; a violent reaction; then shock or a question that ends the chapter without an answer.
4. **Choice:** option one and option two, each with an enticing reward and hidden risk; do not reveal the choice.
5. **Escalating crisis:** a sudden sound or action; a new threat; the protagonist's fearful or urgent reaction.
6. **Imminent identity reveal:** an observer notices a clue; a line hints at the protagonist's real identity; others dismiss it while the protagonist becomes privately tense.
7. **Emotional turn:** a character's expression or attitude changes; an unexpected plea or vulnerable action; a line strikes the protagonist's emotional weak point.
8. **Information gap:** the protagonist's self-doubt or reflection; an image or metaphor; an unstated suggestion that change is coming.
9. **Suspenseful dialogue:** the first half of a request; the other person's response; then an unexpected completion.
10. **Time jump:** a precise or approximate time marker; an unexpected result; one sentence implying the unseen process.

## Three Quick-Sketch Chapter-Ending Hooks

### Reward Preview (End of an Instance/Quest)
```
{当前行动接近完成}。
{暗示即将获得的奖励}。
{一句话暗示奖励比预期更大}。
```

### Approaching Antagonist (End of a Setup Segment)
```
{主角当前状态}。
{暗处/远方，反派的某个行动}。
{暗示这个行动将影响主角}。
```

### Action Preview (Before Showing Off/Comeuppance)
```
{主角做出一个决定}。
{一个准备动作}。
{暗示即将发生的行动}。不写结果。
```

<!-- translation-guide: non-executable -->
**English guide (non-executable):**

- **Reward preview:** the current action nears completion; imply the coming reward; then suggest that it exceeds expectations.
- **Approaching antagonist:** state the protagonist's current condition; show an antagonist acting elsewhere or in secret; imply that the action will affect the protagonist.
- **Action preview:** the protagonist decides; performs a preparation; and signals the coming action without revealing its result.

## Rules for Designing Cathartic Payoffs and Points of Interest

### Core Formula for Cathartic Payoffs

A cathartic payoff is the conflict point between two logics:
- **Conventional logic**: the opponent is unbeatable → fear/despair
- **Protagonist logic**: perfect, I needed a workout / effortless dismissal
- The wider the gap → the more satisfying the payoff

### Three Elements of a Cathartic Payoff

| Role | Operation |
|------|------|
| Onlookers | Use discussion to elevate the opponent's stature → shocked reactions complete and amplify the comeuppance |
| Opponent | Be arrogant enough for readers to hate → then the comeuppance feels satisfying |
| Protagonist | Remain restrained during setup → act decisively at release |

A point of interest raises reader expectation; a cathartic payoff satisfies it. They correspond one-to-one: the amount set up earlier determines the amount available for later payoff.

### Showing-Off and Comeuppance Rhythm

| Stage | Free period (five-chapter cycle) | VIP period |
|------|-------------------|--------|
| Characterization | 1 chapter | 1–2 chapters |
| Setup | 2 chapters | 5 chapters |
| Comeuppance | 1 chapter | 1–2 chapters |
| Aftermath | 1 chapter | 1 chapter |

**Key points**:
- Establish minor invincibility beforehand: before the comeuppance, prepare the prop/person/strength. Without this, readers worry for the protagonist and mockery becomes protagonist abuse
- Setup matters more than the comeuppance: once setup is complete, the protagonist can project dominance merely by standing silently
- The target's status determines the level of satisfaction: shocking an anonymous passerby is not cathartic; shocking someone consequential is
- About 100 Chinese characters of chapter-ending hook can significantly improve completion rate

## Quality Check

After writing a chapter opening or ending, verify:

- [ ] The first 100 Chinese characters contain a hook (not scenery or weather)
- [ ] The chapter ending gives readers a reason to turn the page
- [ ] Hook strength matches the chapter stage (a daily-life chapter does not require a strong hook)
- [ ] Two consecutive chapters do not use the same hook type
- [ ] The hook is about 100 Chinese characters—concise and not prolonged
