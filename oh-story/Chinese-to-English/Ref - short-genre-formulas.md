# Short-Story Genre Formulas

> **Purpose**: Structural frameworks, emotional beats, required scenes, and key rules for short-story genres. Chapter counts are only a method of arranging short sections; they do not represent long-form volume structure.
> **Tone**: Instructional—every formula is a structural framework ready for direct use.
> **Use with**: Prioritize general technique references within the current skill. After story-setup deployment, use the copies of writing techniques, structural formats, and genre-style modules in the project-local agent reference bundle.
> **Scope**: The pacing parameters in this file apply only to short stories and their sections. Transfer long-form work to `story-long-write`; this file provides no long-form opening formula.

## Formula 1: Modern Revenge / Humiliation Short Story (Six Chapters)

| Chapter | Node | Key Point |
|----|------|------|
| 1 | Public betrayal | Protagonist counters calmly on the spot and wins temporarily |
| 2 | Calm response | Freeze or reclaim power and establish the background |
| 3 | Opponent counters | Villain comes to the protagonist; evidence humiliates them |
| 4 | Reveal truth | Surveillance/evidence exposes the villain’s real face |
| 5 | Opponent begs | Show more evidence and overwhelm completely |
| 6 | Final coronation | New order + hidden truth revealed |

**Emotion**: Anger→satisfaction→tension→greater satisfaction→extreme satisfaction→aftertaste + shock
**Required scenes**: Opening public humiliation, terrifyingly calm counterattack, signature action, layered evidence revelation, villain exposes self, hidden setup resolved
**Rules**: Protagonist never loses composure; replace emotion with action; the more hysterical the opponent, the calmer the protagonist; let the villain gloat before the reversal

## Formula 2: Ancient Household Intrigue / Identity Reversal (Eight Sections)

| Section | Node | Key Point |
|----|------|------|
| 1 | Abandoned child returns | Birth parents humiliate the protagonist publicly; protagonist observes coldly |
| 2 | Inner-household clash | Younger sister frames and has the protagonist beaten; hidden track begins |
| 3 | Identity glimpsed | Key figure takes notice; token appears |
| 4 | Confined | Younger sister informs; protagonist falls into desperation |
| 5 | Identity revealed | Imperial decree arrives; everyone is astonished |
| 6 | Counterattack advances | Audit accounts; nobles choose sides |
| 7 | Opponent counters | Rumors + coordinated suppression |
| 8 | Final domination | Imperial audience / full truth; opponents collapse |

**Emotion**: Suppression ×3→release→release→brief renewed suppression→ultimate release
**Required scenes**: Humiliation on return, falsely accused and beaten, token revealed, iconic identity revelation, escalating malice from the sister, increasing parental favoritism
**Rules**: Reveal identity in stages; suppress for three sections and release for one; write parents through “favoritism,” not “evil”; sister’s malice rises from insinuation→framing→attempted murder

## Formula 3: Tragic-Romance Revenge / Spirit POV (Seven Sections)

| Section | Node | Key Point |
|----|------|------|
| 1 | Death recalled | Scumbag caused death three years earlier; spirit POV begins |
| 2 | Abuse scene | Scumbag threatens family; bodyguards attack |
| 3 | Mistress’s hypocrisy | Mistress pretends to be pitiful and tears up the death certificate |
| 4 | Coffin opened / truth | Child says “That is Mommy”; blood-written letter; timed file publication |
| 5 | Public opinion reverses | Trending topic + stock collapse + mistress abandoned |
| 6 | Breakdown | Scumbag chokes mistress; family demands accountability |
| 7 | Resolution | Brother’s plan; scumbag’s final line |

**Emotion**: Suffocation→rage→coldness→heartbreak→satisfaction→catharsis→desolation
**Required scenes**: “Save the child, not the mother”; contrasting scene—family beaten vs. scumbag buying gifts for mistress; one line from the child breaks defenses; timed evidence release; opened coffin; scumbag collapses
**Rules**: Spirit floats overhead; “wants to stop it but cannot”; alternate present with memories from three years ago; scumbag rationalizes his cruelty; every line from the mistress is poisonous

## Formula 6: Fantasy Cultivation / Rebirth Counterattack (19 Chapters)

```
导语[前世被害全貌]→重生回到关键节点→故意不按前世行事→对手自疑自乱
→周围人不解→对手越陷越深→关键人物相助→逐步瓦解反派→最终决战→开放式HE
```

**Emotion**: Frustration→satisfaction→mystery→greater satisfaction→tension→warmth
**Required scenes**: Detailed and brutal previous-life death; first act after rebirth is “abnormal”; opponent’s self-doubt is the core payoff; previous-life truth revealed gradually; influential helper has presence; ending implies romance through negative space
**Rules**: Not repeating previous-life actions = greatest advantage; use abnormal behavior to create information gaps; cultivation serves the romance instead of piling up lore

## Formula 7: Period Rebirth / Dual Revenge (19 Chapters)

```
导语[前世被利用]→双重生→女主选完全不同的路→男主(天阉)温柔登场
→揭发渣男前世罪行→渣男自以为赢→更多证据揭露→渣男崩溃
→男主真实身份揭露→华丽打脸→男主后悔BE收尾
```

**Emotion**: Anger→relief→sweetness→tension→extreme satisfaction→aftertaste
**Required scenes**: Prologue clearly explains previous-life sacrifices; two people reborn and competing through information; period details such as Dacron cloth, ten-yuan notes, and barefoot doctors; male lead appears impaired but is ideal; fully portray scumbag’s collapse; end from his POV with regret
**Rules**: Create period authenticity through real historical details; “scapegoat” has more impact than “cheated on”; dual rebirth = dual information war; scumbag’s despair contrasts with heroine’s freedom

## Formula 8: Palace Intrigue / Empress Counterattack (19 Chapters)

```
家族被害→被迫替嫁→表面顺从暗中布局→带走所有资源离开
→新势力中积蓄力量→发现更大阴谋→联合盟友→反攻京城→旧势力崩溃→称帝+改革+HE
```

**Emotion**: Grief and anger→patience→suspense→satisfaction→extreme satisfaction→fulfillment
**Required scenes**: Family’s destruction is sufficiently brutal; substituted marriage demonstrates intelligence; “taking the family assets” is the turn; every step of the plan has plausible motivation; iconic counterattack on the capital; reforms after coronation demonstrate vision
**Rules**: Protagonist never explains the plan; let readers infer it. Dialogue feels historical but not excessively classical. The plan produces retrospective realization. Villains are cold through self-interest.

## Formula 9: Modern Mystery / Supernatural POV (18 Chapters)

```
设定[鬼伪装成人]→被骗入局→发现怨气能升级→顺水推舟
→揭露犯罪网络→鬼能力升级→对付小喽啰→找幕后黑手
→揭露前世死因→最终对决→法律+超自然双重惩罚
```

**Emotion**: Curiosity→tension→satisfaction→greater satisfaction→shock→catharsis
**Required scenes**: Explain the premise in one sentence; after being deceived, protagonist is “delighted rather than afraid”; spirit POV creates a unique information gap; every upgrade unlocks a previous-life memory; villain reactions create payoffs; legal + supernatural punishment feels better than killing alone
**Rules**: Spirit POV is the unique selling point; “ghosts do not fear evil people—evil people fear ghosts”; interweave mystery and revenge; chapter endings may land on a reversal, new discovery, local fulfillment, or clear next step—do not invent an anomaly merely to satisfy a per-chapter quota

## Formula 10: Alternate History / Gender Reversal (19 Chapters)

```
被家暴/被误解→配偶偏心外人→失望到放弃→决定离开
→配偶不以为然→离开后变好→配偶后悔→配偶疯狂赎罪→主角不原谅→悲剧/开放式
```

**Emotion**: Frustration→deeper frustration→release→ease→quiet satisfaction→lingering regret
**Required scenes**: Falsely accused or beaten in opening; “first-love” rival is convincingly manipulative; protagonist leaves cleanly; spouse’s regret escalates; refusal to forgive is the story’s position; a non-HE ending leaves stronger aftertaste
**Rules**: Gender reversal is the shell; the core is “an unappreciated person chooses to leave.” Every “just endure it” intensifies reader anger. More detailed regret makes the refusal firmer. Tragedy comes from “too late.”

## Formula 12: Rebirth Revenge / Post-Divorce Counterattack (10 Chapters)

```
前世被害→重生到关键节点→冷静接受离婚→拿走应得财富
→投资已知成功项目→系统学习提升→前夫后悔→前夫试图挽回→坚决拒绝→华丽蜕变
```

**Emotion**: Frustration→release→satisfaction→greater satisfaction→extreme satisfaction→fulfillment
**Required scenes**: Previous-life death directly relates to ex-husband; first response after rebirth is “a smile”; divorce is efficient—take money and interests, not the man; investment demonstrates intelligence; ex-husband’s regret escalates; final transformation is concrete
**Rules**: “I know the ending; now I will change the process.” Calm is the greatest weapon. Every decision is supported by previous-life memory. Ex-husband’s flaw is “taking neglect for granted,” not pure evil. Keep investment and romance separate.

## Formula 13: Wealthy CEO / First-Love Tragic Romance (8–11 Chapters)

```
1-2章 虐(建立矛盾)：伤害层层升级(言语→行为→身体)
3-4章 觉醒(心死离开)：临界事件(目睹背叛/身体极限)
5-6章 反转(真相揭露)：监控/证据/白月光自曝
7-8章 惩罚(追悔被拒)：渣男崩溃/反派落败
9-10章 治愈(新生活)：女主独立/新恋情
```

**Emotional ratio**: Suffering 30%→awakening 15%→satisfaction 35%→healing 20%
**Three layers of suffering**: Verbal harm→behavioral harm→physical harm
**Required scenes**: Opening information bomb; every line from the first-love rival humiliates heroine; threshold event—surveillance / mother dies while husband travels with secretary; feigned death or disappearance in 30% of works; scumbag’s pursuit rejected; new romance suggested in 50%
**Six-step synopsis**: Situation→conflict erupts→quotable dialogue→heroine turns→suspense hook→identity preview
**Rules**: 100% first person; harmful dialogue “cuts precisely” rather than merely cursing; awakening happens in an instant; deliver payoffs in order

## Formula 14: Female- vs. Male-Audience Revenge / Humiliation

### Common Female-Audience Path

- **Opening**: First-person “I”; establish the central conflict within three sentences; if rebirth, complete previous-life review within 150 Chinese characters
- **Development**: Escalate villain pressure, protagonist choices, information gaps, and local counters along a causal chain. Adjust only when several consecutive narrative units lack a state change; do not schedule humiliation by character count
- **Climax**: Converge the ultimate truth, identity, or evidence when prior causes support it. Use a public trial only when the observing relationship network amplifies a power reversal
- **Ending**: State the villain’s outcome, protagonist’s choice, and perceptible consequences. Let the warmth section’s length follow the aftermath rather than a fixed count

### Common Male-Audience Path

- **Opening**: Greatest humiliation in the first paragraph; explode without setup; use concrete key data
- **Development**: The period of restraint is usually short; demonstrate ability through action instead of internal speech
- **Counterattack**: Overwhelm through choices, resources, and action. Use a public setting only when witnesses change the outcome
- **Climax**: Converge identity, power, or crucial evidence when prior causes support it. A change in forms of address may prove the relationship reversal; do not force collective astonishment
- **Ending**: After the villain falls, show protagonist choice and aftermath. Let the warmth arc’s length serve the ending’s function

### Core Differences

| Metric | Female Audience | Male Audience |
|------|------|------|
| Emotional-trigger density | 3.23 per 1,000 Chinese characters | 2.50 per 1,000 |
| Reversal density | 1.77 per 1,000 | 1.21 per 1,000 |
| Reversal type | Information gap—inner voice / truth | Power—identity / strength |
| Villain type | Scumbag + manipulative rival + biased parents | Brother + boss + snob |
| Revenge method | Information gap—truth revealed | Power—overwhelming strength |

## Formula 15: Palace Intrigue / Patient Schemer (Eight Chapters)

```
1 主动入局(明知丈夫爱寡嫂仍嫁入)
2 以退为进(比情敌更贤惠→情敌嫉妒失控)
3 借力打力(情敌假晕骗丈夫→丈夫发现被骗→对情敌失望)
4 静待自毁(情敌社交场合闯祸→主角旁观)
5 意外之喜(情敌怀孕→孩子交主角扶养)
6 釜底抽薪(情敌不能再育→主角成为孩子母亲)
7 设局收网(宫宴支开丈夫→情敌杀婆婆→主角"恰好"撞见)
8 终极反转(揭示从头到尾都在操纵→独占家产)
```

**Emotion**: Curiosity→satisfaction from rival’s self-destruction→greater satisfaction from protagonist’s composure→shock at final reversal
**Required scenes**: Abnormal opening behavior; rival self-destructs three times—false fainting→social blunder→kills mother-in-law; every concession is a plan; ending internal monologue reveals truth
**Rules**: Protagonist never attacks directly. After every rival mistake, protagonist appears as “victim” or “good person.” Use ending internal monologue for a perceptual reversal.

## Formula 16: Regretful-Wife Pursuit / No Forgiveness (Eight Chapters)

```
1 发现真相(999次引诱失败→发现暗恋继父)→决定离婚
2 彻底死心(回忆付出→被拒屈辱)→最后的温柔
3 妻子失控(看到继父给别的女人号码后暴怒，但不为男主)
4 被虐(继父绑架男主→妻子和继父开心聊天)→彻底绝望
5 决然离开(妻子以为欲擒故纵→烧了男主东西)
6 丑闻曝光(半年后举报信→继父出卖妻子→身败名裂)
7 重逢(妻子找到男主→发现已有新恋人→眼神陌生)
8 不原谅(妻子跟踪→天台对峙→坚决不回头)
```

**Emotion**: Despair→calm→new life→catharsis→desolation
**Required scenes**: “Nth time” creates escalating despair; discovers truth; wife’s indifference; stepfather betrays wife; new partner creates contrast; rooftop confrontation with BE
**Rules**: The calmer the male lead, the stronger the effect. More detailed regret makes readers believe she deserves it. The new partner proves “someone will cherish me.” Refusing forgiveness has more power than a forced HE.

## Formula 17: Period Rebirth / Medical Revenge (Eight Chapters)

```
1 前世极惨(被500块卖→生育工具→火烧死)→重生
2 金手指展示(银针→给植物人丈夫治疗)→化解前世危机
3 以退为进(面对挑衅不怒→用医术赢得贵人信任)
4 关键转折(丈夫醒来→利用股权要价→获公司股份)
5 步步为营(丈夫保护→逐渐放下心防→感情线推进)
6 收网布局(发现公公被毒杀→丈夫坦白下毒→丈夫也在复仇)
7 复仇高潮(公公葬礼→警察抓大房→大房逃亡)
8 BE但圆满(丈夫癌症去世→复仇成功→孩子和事业留给主角)
```

**Emotion**: Anger→satisfaction→tension→extreme satisfaction→desolation
**Required scenes**: Previous life’s misery escalates; first abnormal response after rebirth—no longer afraid; medical special advantage; influential help; husband reversal—vegetative state was feigned / partly conscious; BE ending
**Rules**: Create period feel through details such as 500 yuan, a marriage intended to dispel bad luck, and acupuncture. Medicine needs professional grounding. Make the senior branch’s cruelty concrete. BE means “gained everything but lost the most important person.”

## Formula 18: Family Tragedy from a Spirit POV (Five Chapters)

```
1 病中被虐(重病被逼做事→身体崩溃→灵魂飘起)
2 旁观真相(灵魂看到家人冷漠→配角拱火→母亲为"面子"变本加厉)
3 证据浮出(父亲发现→手机/日记/监控暴露真相→全家崩溃)
4 审判清算(逐出族谱→配角被惩→反派得报应)
5 重生暖心(投胎温暖家庭→温馨细节呼应前世缺失)
```

**Emotion**: Suffocation→heartbreak→release→catharsis→healing
**Required scenes**: Greatest harm at the body’s weakest point—forced to attend ancestral rites after spinal surgery / forced to cook after a C-section; “my spirit slowly floated upward”; supporting character incites conflict; mother obsessed with “saving face”; phone/diary as evidence; rebirth into a loving family
**Rules**: Spirit can see but cannot intervene. Physical detail must be concrete—the crack of a displaced spine, the rip of a removed back brace. Fully portray the mother’s regret, from “she always pretends” to screaming collapse. End rebirth with contrast; the final sentence must be warm.

### Opening-Line Templates

| Template | Example |
|------|------|
| Forced while ill | “I had just undergone spinal surgery, and the doctor repeatedly ordered three months of rest. But Mom gripped my wrist: Hurry, the ancestral rite is about to begin.” |
| Forced postpartum | “Seven days after my C-section, the incision still ached. My husband opened the door: We have guests today. Go cook a few dishes.” |
| Inhaler taken | “I had just undergone surgery and reached for my rescue inhaler when my cousin snatched it away: Auntie, look—she is pretending to be sick to avoid work again!” |

## Formula 19: Detail-Clue-Driven Revenge

```
1 发现异常(微小细节→冷静应对)
2-3 暗中调查(职业优势系统收集证据→发现更大阴谋)
4-5 布局反击(法律文件→证据陷阱→第三方施压)
6-7 公开对峙(公开场合递交文件→对方崩溃→真相浮出)
8+ 连环反击(对方反扑→逐一破解→主角全新开始)
```

**Emotion**: Alertness→suppression→calm→satisfaction→extreme satisfaction→freedom
**Required scenes**: Triggering detail—key chain / condom brand / receipt / perfume scent; professional advantage; systematic evidence chain; “smiles and nods” moment; turn the opponent’s piece against them; end with “human warmth is expensive”
**Rules**: Section length serves the chain of investigation and counterattack. The protagonist’s composure appears through strategy and choice; losing control may work if it has cause and consequence. Release evidence in stages as judgments change. Use double agents. Escalate betrayal. Describe major emotion coldly.

### Investigation-Chain Template

```
异常物件→搜索确认→实地调查→系统取证(银行/监控/社交)→布局反击→公开审判
```

## Formula 20: Formula-Subverting Framed Rebirth (Seven Chapters)

```
1 重生+假意顺从(知道遗产是陷阱→"你既然想要，就都给你吧")
2 暗中布局(确认继承人身份→准备移民→假意亲近渣男)
3 小试锋芒(不再忍白月光→展示反常行为→制造悬念)
4 冲突升级(被陷害→用条件反击)
5 身份揭露(权威人物→"我沈家家主明明是简希"→对方崩溃)
6 收网(给对方"既要又要"的选择→看他自掘坟墓)
7 华丽退场(揭露所有真相→移民离开→头也不回)
```

**Emotion**: Understanding→satisfaction→greater satisfaction→extreme satisfaction→catharsis→freedom
**Required scenes**: “You can have it” moment; first-love rival tests three times—coaxing→crying→threatening; scumbag wants both options; iconic identity reveal; protagonist watches the opponent self-destruct; leaves “without looking back”
**Rules**: “Giving it to you is the best punishment.” Insert previous-life memories only when they change present judgment, choice, or information—not by chapter quota. Show restraint through concrete choices rather than a fixed “desolate smile.” The greatest contempt is indifference.

## Formula 21: Public-Trial Humiliation

### Core Scene Design

```
1 设定"竞技场"(股东大会/家族寿宴/公司大堂)——必须公开
2 反派先"赢"(当众宣布胜利、羞辱主角)
3 主角标志性冷静(端起水杯/整理西装/笑了笑)
4 逐层揭露(一张一张甩证据)
5 反派逐级崩溃(得意→慌张→绝望→崩溃)
6 标志性驱逐台词
7 背影离场(全场死寂中头也不回)
```

### Signature-Action Library

| Action | Effect |
|------|------|
| Lifts a glass and takes a sip | Terrifying composure |
| Twirls the car keys while strolling inside | A winner’s ease |
| Casually takes a glass of champagne | Elegance on the battlefield |
| Slowly crouches to meet her eyes | Mercy from above |

### Expulsion-Line Library

| Line | Setting |
|------|------|
| “No dogs allowed” | Expelled from the company |
| “From today onward, she may never set foot inside this building again” | Permanent expulsion |

The action and line libraries illustrate only the direction of “calm contrast + declaration of power.” Never copy these sentences: High-frequency stock phrases form a searchable template fingerprint. Invent new ones for each book according to characterization.

## Short-Story Genre Quick Reference

### Social Fiction / Wish Fulfillment

- Deliver humiliation in stages that follow the villain’s arrogance. Check for stagnation when several consecutive narrative units introduce no new wrongdoing, counteraction, or change in cost
- After humiliation, provide evidence gathering or planning before the counterattack; do not delay until readers have forgotten
- Protagonist actively lays a trap or counters decisively
- Villain has internally coherent reasons for wrongdoing
- After the humiliation, sever ties, reclaim assets, or call police cleanly without dragging
- Wish-fulfillment sections are usually short, but length serves the complete action chain. Integrate sensory details into action beats rather than isolating them; show physical responses through plain action

### Emotional / Tragic

- Use concrete objects, numbers, and habits in the first third to establish relationship texture
- Begin with warmth → shatter it with brutal truth
- End with a quiet detail rather than extended lyricism
- Departure needs a clear triggering event
- Show the texture of the new life afterward

### Ancient Romance / Revenge

- Establish relationships in one stroke without complex worldbuilding
- Deliver humiliation directly and decisively
- Reserve the largest trump card for the final quarter
- Use identity or power as a natural trump card
- Match evildoers’ outcomes to their wrongdoing

### Mystery / Deduction

- Arrange information gaps—reader knows / character does not, or vice versa
- Use elimination to discard surface explanations gradually
- Reveal motivation by showing what happened first, then why
- Use rebirth/transmigration as an information-gap tool without expanding its rules
- Every reversal reveals part of the truth and creates a larger question

### Period / Family

- Show both sides’ suffering instead of choosing a side simplistically
- Use period-specific objects and customs to create texture
- Do not rush reconciliation; let both sides be hurt fully
- Severing family ties includes repetition and struggle
- Fill the rupture with new relationships

### Pull-Strength Levels

> Strength describes genre expectations, not a per-section quota. Place hooks where reader expectations, action direction, or relationship judgments change. Review only when several consecutive sections raise questions without fulfillment.

| Genre | Pull Strength | Preference |
|------|----------|------|
| Revenge / wish fulfillment | Extreme | Humiliation, trump cards, cost |
| Mystery | Strong | Information gaps, countdowns, reversals |
| Emotional / tragic | Moderately strong | Emotion, contrast, aftertaste |
| Ancient romance / palace intrigue | Moderately strong | Trump cards, humiliation, cost |
| Period / family | Moderate | Emotion, contrast, aftertaste |

## Quality-Control Checklist

> Check every item after writing with a formula. The result passes only when all succeed.

- [ ] **Formula alignment**: The formula matches the genre label without mixing unrelated formulas
- [ ] **Complete emotional beats**: The emotional curve follows the formula’s beats without skipping or changing order
- [ ] **All required scenes**: Every “required scene” appears
- [ ] **Core rules followed**: Every rule in the formula is implemented
- [ ] **Length compliant**: Chapter/section count falls within the formula’s range
- [ ] **Female-/male-audience distinction**: Revenge/humiliation fiction is adjusted according to Formula 14’s central differences
- [ ] **Unbroken pull**: Hooks land where expectations, action direction, or relationship judgments change; no run of sections only asks questions without fulfillment
- [ ] **Opening grabs in three seconds**: The first paragraph or sentence establishes the central conflict or an information bomb without slow setup
