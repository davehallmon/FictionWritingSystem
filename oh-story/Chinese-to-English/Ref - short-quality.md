# Short-Form Quality Coverage

> Use with `agent-quality.md` and load only for the `short` profile. Review the complete story and each section. Long-form requirements—such as the opening three chapters, volume-level tension and release, and payoff cycles spanning more than a dozen chapters—do not apply.

## Overall Density and Payoffs

- [ ] Every section changes at least one of the following: information, relationships, risk, objectives, ownership of evidence, or emotional position. Do not mechanically add conflict every 300 Chinese characters.
- [ ] Use only one central mystery. Secondary mysteries support evidence, misdirection, or emotional cost; they must not expand into separate stories.
- [ ] Every delayed answer delivers new evidence, corrects a misjudgment, fractures a relationship, or provides a partial counterattack. Do not spend two consecutive sections merely escalating the question.
- [ ] Leave room to establish and resolve the largest emotional or cognitive payoff. Treat 70–85% only as a common planning range for revenge or suspense—not as a rigid cross-genre threshold.
- [ ] Place the paywall at an unfinished action, a change in evidence ownership, an identity anomaly, or a dilemma. Do not rely on unsupported declarations of danger.

## Reversals and Evidence Chains

- [ ] Before every layer of reversal, provide at least one traceable clue. Misdirecting information must itself be true; only its apparent interpretation is wrong.
- [ ] The first layer provides a credible provisional answer. The second both explains earlier clues and changes the emotional judgment.
- [ ] Release evidence in batches, with the final batch changing the reader's global understanding. Let story length and genre determine the number of batches.
- [ ] After the reveal, allow actions, relationships, or costs to resolve. Do not explain every mechanism through a long monologue.
- [ ] If a deeper conflict or second issue appears in the latter half, connect it to the opening problem through at least two prior causal or evidentiary links. Otherwise, the story has switched central plots rather than escalated.
- [ ] Build the evidence chain as a ladder: “source or physical evidence → contest over interpretation → reversal of ownership or responsibility.” Every rung changes a character's next action instead of merely adding background.

## Dialogue and Character

- [ ] Dialogue performs evidence gathering, shifts power, fractures relationships, or exploits information gaps. Its density follows the genre and source-text baseline; do not impose a universal 45–65% range.
- [ ] A protagonist's composure, loss of control, or forcefulness must follow from characterization and circumstances. Do not require stock signature actions such as lifting a cup or spinning a pen.
- [ ] The antagonist or manipulator has an independent motive and does not abruptly acquire new traits solely to enable the final reversal.
- [ ] For every decisive evidentiary turn, identify who obtains it, who verifies it, and who chooses to disclose it. The protagonist must own at least one decisive discovery and the final irreversible choice rather than receiving every answer from supporting characters.

## Review Output Contract

- Every blocking and high-risk finding must include traceable evidence, the consequence of failure, and the smallest viable fix. Offer one optional alternative only when meaningfully different directions exist.
- The smallest viable fix must identify which section to change, which causal link to add, or which resolution to move. Do not write only “strengthen the setup” or “increase agency.”
- Consolidate similar symptoms under one root cause. Do not use a large issue count as a substitute for analytical depth.

## Ending

- [ ] Resolve the central causal and emotional accounts. Do not open a new long-form plotline in the final section.
- [ ] End on an action, object, relationship choice, or visible consequence. Resonance is not an authorial summary.
- [ ] Let the story's actual unresolved obligations determine the length of post-reversal explanation and reckoning; do not require a fixed 500–800 Chinese characters.
