## Cross-Platform Differences in Short-Fiction Writing

| Dimension | Qimao | Heiyan | Dianzhong | Zhihu |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| Primary word count | 10,000–20,000 Chinese characters | 8,000–40,000 Chinese characters | 10,000–20,000 Chinese characters (10 chapters × 1,000) | 5,000–15,000 Chinese characters (median 12,000) |
| Number of chapters | 7–19 | 4–14 sections (or one 40,000-character chapter) | 5–10 (standard: 10 chapters) | 6–10 |
| Point of view | First person: 95%+ | First person: 85% (women's) / 50% (men's) | First person: 95%+ | Mixed (historical romance uses third person) |
| Chapter length | 1,000–1,500 Chinese characters | 2,000–5,000 Chinese characters | 800–1,500 Chinese characters | 800–1,500 Chinese characters |
| Dialogue share | 20–38% (mean 27.7%) | 30–50% | 9% average (functional) | 20–40% (many quotable lines) |
| Paragraph structure | 1–3 sentences/paragraph | 1–3 sentences/paragraph | 1–2 sentences/paragraph (16–37 Chinese characters) | 1–3 sentences/paragraph (30–60 Chinese characters) |
| Lead/synopsis | Required (100–200 Chinese characters) | Required (100–363 Chinese characters) | Required (150–300 Chinese characters) | The synopsis is the work (200–500 Chinese characters) |
| Opening pattern | Rebirth flashback / conflict brought forward | Extreme shock / moral violation | Title states the plot / extreme incident | One-sentence bombshell / quotable opening |
| Ending pattern | Mainly HE (50%+ new romance) | HE/BE evenly split | Mainly HE (regret/public humiliation) | Diverse (many open endings) |

| Gratification pacing | Abuse 30% → awakening 15% → gratification 35% → healing 20% | Women's: small reversal every 300–500 characters / men's: major explosion every 1,000–2,000 | Small turn every 3 chapters / major explosion in Chapter 10 | Layered revelations / slow pace with lasting impact |
| Emotional density | Medium–high | Highest (women's: 3.23 events per 1,000 characters) | Extremely high (short-sentence share: 33–66%) | Medium (restrained but forceful) |
| Dialogue quotation marks | 「」 or "" | 「」 or "" | Primarily "" | Primarily 「」 |
| Core positioning | Rebirth fantasy | Extreme stimulation | Fast emotional consumption | Premium emotional fiction |

## Platform-Specific Synopsis Formulas

| Platform | Synopsis formula | Length | Core trait |
|------|---------|------|---------|
| Qimao CEO romance | Normal life → harmful incident → cruelest dialogue → heroine's reversal → suspense hook | 133–322 Chinese characters | 70% include dialogue; choose the most hurtful line |
| Heiyan women's fiction | Escalating accusation / thoughts exposed / terminal-illness countdown / rebirth contrast / public humiliation + hidden card | 103–363 Chinese characters | The core is “this time, I...” |
| Heiyan men's fiction | Minimal dialogue + information bombshell / murdered in previous life + rebirth / workplace exploitation + supernatural element | 109–401 Chinese characters | Open directly with the most explosive scene or dialogue |
| Dianzhong | Scene setup + trust trap + fatal truth + hint of counterattack | 150–300 Chinese characters | Condense the strongest material from the first third |
| Zhihu | Character relationship + core conflict + 2–3 turns + quotable line/negative space | 200–500 Chinese characters | The synopsis is a standalone micro-story ending on a quotable line |

## Distinctive Platform Traits

### Qimao

- First person: 95%+; female protagonists: 80%+
- 1,000–1,500 Chinese characters per chapter; 7–10 chapters is the primary range
- Nearly all works have an explicit lead (a preface summarizing the story's central conflict)

### Zhihu

- Rich professional detail (medical knowledge in medical stories, legal detail in legal stories)
- Focus on social issues (in-law conflict, son preference, medical disputes)
- More restrained emotion with greater lingering impact; more realistic dialogue
- The synopsis is the work: a 200–500-character micro-story ending on a quotable line

### Dianzhong Title Formulas

- `白眼狼妹妹把家产送人，我让她净身出户` = character + action + counterattack
- `愚人节死在棺材里后，三个哥哥都悔疯了` = incident + consequence + emotion
- `春节返程，老公一家把我女儿挂车外狗笼子里` = setting + conflict + anger
