## Platform Selection and Positioning

### Mainstream Platform Comparison

| Platform | Traits | Suitable conditions |
|------|------|----------|
| Qidian | Flagship platform, subscription model, mature recommendation system | Long-running men's fiction driven by follow-reading, with a main plot and gratification loop that can keep expanding |
| Tomato Novel | Free reading, heavy traffic, strongly oriented toward completion and tags | Attention-grabbing title, fast pace, strong emotion or high-concept premise; use current rankings to verify the risk of AI homogenization |
| Qimao | Mass-market audience, strong emotional narratives | Strong romantic line, sweet/angst push-pull, high emotional density; contract and revenue rules must follow the latest official terms |
| Jinjiang | Primary venue for women's fiction, danmei, and romance | Prioritize when agency, reassuring copy, character detail, and metrics such as favorites/nutrient solution can support the work |

### Selection Principles

- Match the genre to platform readers before comparing traffic; do not equate platform scale directly with a project's likelihood of success.
- Revenue judgments involving contracts, minimum guarantees, revenue share, or attendance bonuses must follow the latest official platform rules or editor-confirmed information. This file supplies only fit dimensions.
- Primary-platform and distribution-channel candidates may be retained simultaneously, but list the target readers, genre samples, core metrics, and failure risks for each platform.
- Platform selection must examine at least three directions: prose pacing, genre hook, and sample fit.

### Women's-Fiction Submission Preferences

| Platform | Submission preferences |
|------|----------|
| Qimao | Strong emotional narratives with alternating sweetness and anguish and extreme push-pull; protective older-style male lead vs. great beauty, groveling-after-breakup romance, 1960s–1990s sweet romance + foil group, or multi-male-lead romantic rivalry |
| Qidian Women's | Before treating it as a candidate, recheck recent rankings and recommendation-slot samples. If samples in the target genre are sparse, mark it as high traffic risk |

## Recommendation Mechanisms

### Qidian Recommendation Flow

```
出库 -> 试水期(7天) -> 培育期(21-42天) -> 三江推荐/上架
```

**Trial period**: New books from the same cohort share one traffic pool, with constant total traffic over seven days.

**Cultivation period**: Four levels: traffic package++ > traffic package+ > traffic package > support package (consolation tier). One traffic package arrives every 1–2 days, for 21 packages total. The size of the next package depends on the previous package's performance (follow-reading + retention). A strong hook with poor retention creates a vicious cycle.

**Key data**:
- 300 simulated follow-readers at release can bridge seamlessly into the trial period.
- At least 500 simulated follow-readers are needed to compete for Sanjiang.
- Below 1,000 simulated follow-readers, all packages are support packages; above 2,000, traffic package+ becomes available.

### Qidian New-Book Ranking Rules

A work leaves the ranking upon meeting any one of three conditions: 200,000 Chinese characters / 30 days since signing / more than 3 days without an update.

**Technique for staying on the ranking longer**: After an internal submission is accepted, coordinate with the editor to publish roughly 30,000 Chinese characters before completing the signing process, which can add 4–5 days on the ranking.

### Tomato Novel Recommendation Mechanism

- Recommendation checkpoints, test lengths, and traffic-package rules change frequently. Before execution, verify them in the official creator dashboard, through an editor, or in the latest author announcement.
- When analyzing Tomato Novel samples, record validation-period length, current readers, completion/follow-up rate, title tags, and synopsis hook.
- If the current scan finds many AI-homogenized samples, report homogenization risk and differentiation checks instead of directly reusing historical traffic-allocation numbers.
- When observing data, set review points using the project's actual length checkpoints and the platform's latest rules; do not use fixed lengths as hard rules.

### Importance of Title and Synopsis

Under Qidian's new recommendation mechanism, title and synopsis are the first filter:
- A title that identifies the genre protects the performance floor.
- Title/synopsis acquisition >> follow-reading >> completion rate/bookshelf ratio.
- A literary title lengthens the cultivation period under the new mechanism.

## Interpreting Data Metrics

### Core Tomato Novel Data

**Follow-up readers** = valid readers who read chapters updated within the last 7 days. Compare this metric only against samples in the same genre, length range, and recommendation stage.

| Metric | Use |
|------|------|
| Follow-up readers | Judge recent reader retention and anticipation for updates; the measurement period must be stated |
| Current readers | Judge traffic scale in conjunction with book age and recommendation stage |
| Completion/completion rate | Identify where readers drop between chapters; if unavailable, mark it missing in the report |
| Bookshelf/favorites | Judge whether acquired readers remain; analyze together with the title and synopsis |

Historical follow-up ranges may be used only as sample annotations. Do not predict a fixed debut volume without current same-stage platform data.

### Core Qidian Data

- **Favorite-to-first-subscription ratio**: The ratio of favorites to first subscriptions, reflecting conversion efficiency.
- **24-hour follow-reading**: Real-time follow-reading by VIP readers; this metric determines recommendation placement.
- **First-subscription reference**: Fewer than 100 first subscriptions at launch is mid-tier performance.

### General Data Logic

- High completion rate = good content quality; high follow-reading = strong anticipation.
- A good bookshelf ratio but poor completion = a problem in the middle content.
- Revenue relates to follow-reading/current-reader metrics, but actual revenue depends on platform rules, contract terms, and recommendation stage and must be verified separately.
- When data performs poorly, first inspect opening acquisition, chapter retention, and gaps in anticipation.

## Writing the Synopsis

Core structure: protagonist's situation → what the special advantage can do → preview of the first gratification beat. Write an advertisement, not a setting manual. State benefits concretely.

**Common mistakes**: disconnected sentences with no central theme | a creative name for what is actually an ordinary points system | empty clichés such as “watch how the protagonist...” | first-person self-reference such as “俺” that breaks immersion.

**Extracting synopsis structure**: Consult Sanjiang recommendations (qidian.com/sanjiang/) or bestselling synopses in the same genre. Extract only functional positions: problem, benefit, gratification preview, reassurance, and suspense. Do not reproduce specific plots or phrasing.
