# Style Standards for Story-Decomposition Reports

> **Purpose:** Applies only to the analyst's own prose in `拆文报告.md`. Do not use these rules to rewrite or filter the source text.
> Clichés, AI-like phrasing, and reader-repelling elements in the source are valid findings. Preserve quoted source text exactly and identify its location.

## Core Principles

A story-decomposition report should read like a reproducible analytical record: state the finding first, then provide textual evidence, its location, and its effect. Do not turn the report into narrative prose or use “polishing” as a pretext for inventing material that is absent from the source.

Every important finding must contain at least three elements:

1. **Conclusion:** Identify the structure, technique, or failure pattern.
2. **Evidence:** Provide a short quotation, plot event, or traceable location.
3. **Effect:** Explain how it changes what the reader knows, expects, or feels.

When evidence is insufficient, write “Insufficient evidence / Requires verification.” Do not confidently invent the author's intent.

## High-Risk Language in the Report

Review the following patterns when they appear in the analyst's prose. Direct quotations from the source do not count:

- Formulaic negation-and-reversal constructions such as “not A, but B” or “not A, not B, only C.”
- Empty signposting such as “it is not difficult to see,” “obviously,” “without question,” or “it is worth noting.”
- Narrative-style elevation or foreshadowing such as “the gears of fate,” “a greater storm,” or “the story has only just begun.”
- Unsupported all-purpose evaluations such as “to the fullest,” “layer by layer,” “tightly interlocking,” or “suffocating.”
- Repeatedly fragmenting a direct observation with “first / second / next / finally.”
- Applying labels such as “tight pacing,” “three-dimensional characters,” or “rich emotion” without explaining the specific textual mechanism.
- Speculating about the author's psychology: “the author wants to tell us” or “the author clearly realizes.” Replace this with an observable textual effect.

## Recommended Sentence Patterns

- `第 3 节先让读者知道证据存在，但隐去持有人；第 5 节才揭示持有人，因此形成两拍信息差。`
- `该段被归为关系反转，证据是母亲在前文始终回避称谓，而此处第一次直呼姓名。`
- `未发现足够铺垫：反转前只有结果提示，没有可回溯的行为线索。`
- `这句属于源文引用，不参与报告自身的表达检查。`

## Pre-Delivery Checklist

- [ ] Every central finding can be traced to the source text, a plot event, or a statistical result.
- [ ] Direct source quotations are clearly distinguished from the analyst's prose.
- [ ] Genre formulas are not treated as sufficient answers; the report explains how the source follows, departs from, or adapts each formula.
- [ ] Speculation is not presented as fact; uncertain items are marked “Insufficient evidence / Requires verification.”
- [ ] Negation-and-reversal formulas, empty signposting, generic adjectives, and narrative-style elevation have been removed from the report's own prose.
- [ ] The report does not invent authorial intent, reader reactions, or missing plot events merely to appear complete.
