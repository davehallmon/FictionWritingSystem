## Core Salt-Selection Conventions

| Feature | Rule |
|------|------|
| Viewpoint | Limited first person; no omniscient description |
| Dialogue format | Use `「」`; give dialogue its own paragraph and keep speaker tags concise |
| Sentence patterns | Combine long and short sentences. Narrative should rely primarily on longer comma-linked sentences, with short sentences reserved for emotional turning points. Do not fragment individual sentences or use a telegraphic style throughout |
| Layout | Begin each paragraph with two full-width spaces, use no blank lines between paragraphs, and use one blank line between chapters |

## Salt-Selection Decomposition Checklist

| Check | Description |
|--------|------|
| Viewpoint consistency | First person throughout; identify any viewpoint shifts |
| Dialogue format | Use `「」` consistently without mixing quotation-mark styles |
| Sentence rhythm | Long and short sentences alternate appropriately: narrative primarily uses longer comma-linked sentences, while short sentences occur only at emotional turning points; avoid a consistently telegraphic style |
| Conversational quality | Identify any remaining overly literary or translation-like phrasing |
| Emotional pacing | Confirm a clear emotional curve; Salt-Selection stories depend heavily on emotional tension and release |
| Reversal placement | The reversal occurs only when prior causes and evidence can support it, while leaving enough space for character choices and consequences; percentage placement is recorded only as descriptive data |
| Chapter-end continuity | Record unfinished actions, new information, partial payoffs, relationship changes, resonance, or a clear next step; do not require every chapter to use the same hook |

## Salt-Selection Genres and Emotional Patterns

| Genre | Core emotion | Typical reversal |
|------|----------|----------|
| Tragic romance | Lingering regret | He had loved her all along |
| Rebirth | Cathartic vindication | This time, the protagonist refuses to repeat the same mistake |
| Suspense | Retrospective dread | The killer has been nearby all along |
| Workplace | Righteous anger and recognition | The counterattack succeeds |
| Marriage | Recognition plus healing | Life improves after leaving the relationship |
| Healing | Warmth | Previously overlooked love is discovered |

## Platform Style Differences

| Dimension | Zhihu Salt Selection | Fanqie Short Fiction | Xiaohongshu Stories |
|------|----------|----------|------------|
| Viewpoint | Limited first person | Unrestricted | Unrestricted |
| Length | 8,000–20,000 Chinese characters | Flexible | 1,000–5,000 Chinese characters |
| Primary appeal | Emotional tension plus reversals | Rapid payoff pacing | Topical relevance plus emotional recognition |
| Dialogue style | Salt-Selection `「」` quotation marks | Standard quotation marks | Flexible |
| Reader expectation | Emotional pain followed by a healing reversal | A satisfying story consumed in one sitting | A relatable topic worth sharing |
| Monetization | Membership or per-story purchase | Free reading | Free discovery funnel |
